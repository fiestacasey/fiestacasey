### Hi, I'm Casey 👋

I'm the technical co-founder and Chief Software Architect at [Wantable](https://www.wantable.com), a personal styling service. I was the first employee in 2012, built the platform from the first prototype, and have led the technology team ever since: engineers, product managers, data scientists and security folks.

**Things I've built**

- **Wantable**: the commerce, styling, subscription and fulfillment platform behind wantable.com. Grew from prototype to 10K+ orders a week and an Inc. 5000 ranking (#223). Lately I've been building internal AI tools on top of it for our stylists and staff.
- **Wantable Shopify stores**: the Wantable shop and our SEAS brand store, connected to our own order and fulfillment systems.
- **[ShipPulse](https://ship-pulse.com)**: a Shopify app I built solo. Delivery date estimates that learn from each store's shipping history, plus a free shipping progress bar. [On the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/shippulse).
- **[Fluency](https://fluencypay.ai)**: an AI-powered text messaging platform for creators and brands, with LLM personas and Stripe Connect payments. Launched at New York Fashion Week with 17 Shopify brands.

**Open source**

A few gems we extracted from the Wantable codebase: [omniship](https://github.com/wantable/omniship) (shipping carrier APIs), [card_connect_gateway](https://github.com/wantable/card_connect_gateway) (payment gateway client) and [ar_state_machine](https://github.com/wantable/ar_state_machine) (an Active Record state machine).

**What I work in**

Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL, Redis, JavaScript (Stimulus, Tailwind), Heroku, Cloudflare, the Shopify and Stripe APIs, and the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs.

Most of my work lives in private repos, so the contribution graph tells only part of the story. 📍 Milwaukee, WI
